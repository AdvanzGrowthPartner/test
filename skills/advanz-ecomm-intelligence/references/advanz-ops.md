## Target Niches
1. Salud / Suplementos / Longevidad — consumible, alto LTV, stack
2. Bienestar / Wellness — lifestyle, comunidad
3. Moda deportiva — brand-love, UGC, seasonal
4. Mascotas premium — highest retention, suscripción natural
5. Skincare natural — rutinas expandibles, consumible
Excluded: commodities, low-recompra, no community potential.

## Core Principles
1. Diagnose before prescribe (signal tables + diagnostics in every module)
2. Engine is a loop (E8 feedback → E1 refinement → continuous improvement)
3. Retention before scale (repeat <15% = don't scale, fix the bucket first)
4. LATAM ≠ US (higher trust barriers, lower CPMs, WhatsApp mandatory, different payments)
5. GEO + SEO (both traditional and AI search required 2026+)
6. Earned > Owned for AI (third-party mentions > brand content for LLM citation)
7. Creative = 80% of Meta success (audience/structure matter less than stopping the scroll)
8. Email flows = 24/7 salespeople (highest ROI per hour in ecommerce)
