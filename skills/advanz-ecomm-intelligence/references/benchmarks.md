# Ecommerce Benchmarks & KPIs — 2025-2026
Sources: Triple Whale 2025 (33K+ brands, $18.4B spend), Klaviyo 2025, Baymard Institute, Build Grow Scale 2026 (2,654+ stores), EMARKETER 2026, Princeton GEO Research, Adobe Analytics, WordStream 2025, Gartner 2026.

## Conversion Rate (CVR)
Global ecommerce avg: 2.5-3%. Shopify new stores: 1.4%. Top performers: 4-5%+.
By traffic source: Email 5%+, Organic search 3%+, Google Search brand 5-12%, Google non-brand 2-4%, Google Shopping/PMax 1.5-3%, Meta cold 0.5-1.5%, Meta retargeting 3-5%.
By industry (BGS 2026): Health & Beauty 3.2%, Food & Beverage 3.5%, Sports & Outdoors 2.8%, Pets 2.5%, Fashion 2.0%.
Each +0.5% CVR improvement = massive revenue impact. Prioritize CVR over traffic at any store doing <3%.

## AOV (Average Order Value)
Median paid traffic (TW): $74 USD. Health & Beauty $55-80. Sports $65-90. Pets $45-70. Supplements $50-75. Skincare $55-85.
Optimization levers: Bundles (+15-25%), BNPL/cuotas (+20-40%), free shipping threshold (+10-15%), post-purchase upsell (+10-20%), cross-sell in cart (+5-10%).

## Meta Ads (2025)
ROAS median 1.93x, top 3-5x. CPA median $38, top <$18. CPM $10-14 (US $20+), LATAM $3-8. CTR 2.19%, top 3.5%+. CVR 1.57%, top 3%+. CPC $1.07, top <$0.50. LATAM CPC $0.30-0.80.
By vertical CPM: Health/Beauty $11-14, Sports $10-13, Pets $8-11, Wellness $9-12, Fashion $10-14.
Sports & Outdoors has highest ROAS at 2.28x. 68% of ecomm ad spend goes to Meta.
CRITICAL: ROAS 1.93x ≠ profitable. At 60% margin, break-even ROAS = 1.67x. Only 15% above break-even.

## Google Ads (2025)
Search Brand: ROAS 8-15x, CPC $0.30-0.80, CVR 5-12%. Search Non-brand: ROAS 2-3x, CPC $1-2.50, CVR 2-4%. Shopping/PMax: ROAS 2-4x, CPC $0.50-1, CVR 1.5-3%. Remarketing: ROAS 4-8x.
WARNING: Brand ROAS 8-15x is misleading — cannibalizes organic. Measure non-brand separately.

## Email/SMS (Klaviyo 2025)
Abandoned Cart: RPR $3.65 (top 10% $28.89), placed order rate 3.33%, open rate 41-52%.
Welcome: RPR $2.65 (top 10% $21.18), placed order rate 1.97%, open rate 45-55%.
Browse Abandonment: RPR $1.07 (top 10% $7.21), open rate 35-45%.
Post-Purchase: RPR $0.41 (top 10% $5.14), open rate 59-62%.
Win-back: RPR $0.84, open rate 30-40%.
Email revenue share: mature 20-30%, top 30-40%. If <15% = flows broken/absent.
Popup submit rate top brands: 3%+.

## Cart Abandonment (Baymard)
Global rate: 70%. Causes: unexpected costs 48%, required account 26%, long checkout 25%, can't see total 22%, don't trust site 18%.
Recovery: no flow 0%, basic 3 emails 3-5%, optimized multi-channel 5-10%, top 10% ~7% placed order rate.

## Retention
Repeat rate 12m: good 20-30%, excellent 35%+. Revenue from recurrents: mature 40-50%.
LTV:CAC: <2:1 danger, 3:1 healthy, 5:1+ scale aggressively.
By niche repeat rate: Supplements 30-40% (45%+), Sportswear 20-25% (30%+), Pets 35-45% (50%+), Skincare 25-35% (40%+).
Time buy 1→2: Supplements 30-45d, Skincare 45-75d, Pets 25-40d, Sportswear 90-120d.

## GEO / AI Search (2026)
31.3% US uses AI search (EMARKETER). 60% searches zero-click. AI traffic to retail +4,700% YoY (Adobe Jul 2025). Cited brands: +38% clicks, +39% ad performance. Structured content 28-40% more likely cited (Princeton). Gartner: -25% organic traffic by end 2026. GEO results 30-60 days (faster than SEO).

## Social Media Organic (2025-2026)
Instagram avg engagement rate: 1.5-3% (good), 5%+ (excellent). Reels reach 2-3x static posts. UGC ad creatives CTR 20-50% higher than produced. TikTok organic reach still highest of platforms but declining. LinkedIn B2B: 2-5% engagement rate. Reddit: 100M+ DAU, one of most-cited LLM sources.

## Key Formulas
Break-even ROAS = 1 / Gross Margin. CPA Max = AOV × Gross Margin. RPV = CVR × AOV. MER = Total Revenue / Total Ad Spend. LTV:CAC = Customer LTV / CAC. Replenishment timing = cycle × 0.8.
