# Conversion Optimization — CRO, PDP, Pricing, AOV, Trust, Social Proof

## PDP (Product Detail Page) Framework

### Above the Fold (must include)
Title with primary keyword. Rating + review count visible. Price + BNPL/cuotas option. Hero image (lifestyle + product shots, min 5 images). CTA button ("Agregar al carrito"). Shipping time + return policy badge. Trust badges (secure payment, guarantee).

### Copy Structure: PAS
Problem: name the pain the avatar recognizes. Agitate: amplify consequences of not solving. Solution: product as the answer. Benefits > features. Lead with what the customer GETS, not what the product HAS.

### Below the Fold
Detailed benefits (not just features). Ingredients/materials with explanation. Social proof section (reviews with photos). FAQ with Schema markup. Cross-sell recommendations. Full return/guarantee policy.

### PDP Optimization Checklist
Images: 5+ per product. Mix of lifestyle (in use) + studio (detail) + scale (size reference). Video if possible (+20-30% CVR lift).
Copy: unique per PDP. Not supplier copy. PAS structure with keywords.
Reviews: min 10 before scaling ads. 4.2+ stars. With photos. Social proof visible above fold.
Trust: payment badges, guarantee, return policy, shipping time. All visible without scrolling.
BNPL: visible next to price ("or X cuotas of $Y"). +20-40% AOV lift.
Mobile: 70%+ traffic is mobile. Test on phone, not desktop. CTA visible without scrolling. Images fast-loading.

## Pricing & AOV Strategy

### Pricing Architecture
Hero product: competitive price, high margin. Entry point to the brand.
Bundles: hero + complementary. 15-20% off vs individual. +15-25% AOV.
Premium: higher tier of hero (larger size, better formula, complete kit). 25-40% more expensive.
Subscription: 10-15% off recurring. Free shipping. Easy cancel.

### AOV Optimization Tactics
1. Bundle offers (+15-25% AOV): "Complete your routine" or "Stack completo".
2. Free shipping threshold (+10-15%): set at 1.3× current AOV. Progress bar in cart.
3. BNPL/cuotas (+20-40%): reduce perceived cost. Show per-installment price on PDP.
4. Post-purchase upsell (+10-20%): 1-click upsell on thank you page. Complementary product, time-limited discount.
5. Cross-sell in cart (+5-10%): "People also bought" or "Complete your order with".
6. Volume discounts (+10-15%): "Buy 2, save 10%" or "Buy 3, get 1 free".
7. Gift with purchase: low-cost high-perceived-value item above threshold.

### Pricing Psychology
Price anchoring: show "compare at" or original price. 9-ending (not $50, but $49). Per-unit pricing for multipacks. Per-day pricing for subscriptions ("less than $1.50/day"). Never compete on price alone in brand niches — compete on value, quality, and trust.

## Trust Signals — 8 Imprescindibles (order of impact)

1. Return policy (clear + generous): header banner + PDP + checkout + footer. 48% abandon due to unclear costs/policies. 30-60 day minimum. Longer guarantee = LOWER return rate (counterintuitive).
2. Recognized payment methods: Webpay + Mercado Pago in Chile. PayPal/Apple Pay globally. If they don't see THEIR method, they leave.
3. BNPL / installments: visible next to price. +40% AOV.
4. SSL + payment security badge: next to credit card field in checkout. Critical for unknown brands.
5. Explicit guarantee: "If it doesn't work, full refund." PDP + email post-purchase.
6. Clear shipping times: PDP + checkout. Biggest complaint in LATAM = delivery uncertainty.
7. Real contact (WhatsApp): header + footer. In LATAM, WA is mandatory. Response <1h.
8. About Us with real photos: real team, real story, real values. Converts better than discounts for brand-love niches.

## Social Proof System

### Hierarchy (by impact)
1. Reviews with customer photo/video (UGC) — most credible, authentic.
2. Number of sales/customers — volume as trust signal.
3. Star ratings with text reviews — the standard, min 4.2 stars.
4. Named testimonials with location — more credible than anonymous.
5. Certifications and press badges — external authority.
6. Real-time activity ("3 people viewing") — use carefully, fake = destroys trust.

### Minimums Before Scaling Ads to a PDP
0 reviews = budget burned. 1-9 = low-budget test only. 10-30 = baseline for scaling. 50+ = sweet spot. 100+ = competitive advantage. Rating <4.0 = reviews HURT more than help.

### Getting First 20 Reviews Fast (no cheating)
1. Automated post-purchase flow with incentive (30-40% leave review with incentive).
2. Personal email to 10-20 best customers (60-80% respond when personal).
3. Physical card in packaging with QR to review (10-15%).
4. Import reviews if migrating from another platform.
5. Gift product to 10 micro-influencers for honest review.

### Responding to Negatives: ACE Framework
Acknowledge: empathy, no excuses. Correct: concrete action taken. Extend: take private to resolve.
NEVER: ignore, argue publicly, make excuses, offer public discount (incentivizes negative reviews), or delete legit reviews. A well-handled negative review builds MORE trust than 5 positive ones.

## Checkout Optimization
The 2 biggest killers (74% of abandonments combined): unexpected costs + forced account creation.
Fix #1: show total cost BEFORE checkout. Progress bar for free shipping in cart.
Fix #2: enable guest checkout ALWAYS. Offer account creation AFTER purchase.
Fix #3: max 3 steps. Shopify default checkout is good — don't add unnecessary fields.
Fix #4: shipping calculator visible in cart (before checkout).
Fix #5: security badges NEXT TO card input field.
