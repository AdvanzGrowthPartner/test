# Funnels, Business Models & Community

## Ecommerce Funnel Architecture

### TOFU → MOFU → BOFU
TOFU (awareness): content, social media, broad ads, blog, GEO. Goal: make them aware the problem exists.
MOFU (consideration): retargeting, email nurture, comparisons, guides, social proof. Goal: make your brand the preferred solution.
BOFU (decision): abandoned cart recovery, offers, urgency, guarantees, checkout optimization. Goal: remove final friction.

### The Complete Ecommerce Funnel
Meta Ad (cold) → Landing/PDP → Browse (ViewContent) → Add to Cart → Checkout → Purchase → Post-purchase flow → Cross-sell → Replenishment → Loyalty → Referral.
Each step has a drop-off rate. Each drop-off is an optimization opportunity. The biggest ROI is fixing the step with the HIGHEST absolute drop.

## Challenge Funnel (for lead gen / community)
Ad → Landing page → 5-7 day Challenge (via WhatsApp/Telegram) → Community invitation → Paid tier upsell.
Why WhatsApp delivery: 70-80% completion rate vs 3-5% traditional courses. Zero login friction. App they already use 40+ times/day.
Content for challenge: diagnostic checklists, signal tables, quick wins from Growth Engine modules.
Challenge pricing: $0 (lead magnet) or $19-49 (paid, qualifies higher intent).
Challenge → Community conversion: 60-70% (warm audience, trust built, got result).

## DIY / DWY / DFY Agency Model

### Value Ladder
FREE: community, sample content, 1 monthly Q&A → ATTRACT volume.
DIY ($49-79/mo): full course access, templates, community, weekly Q&A → SELF-SERVICE, filters who needs more.
DWY ($297-497/mo): all DIY + group coaching 2x/mo, hot seats, premium templates, private channel → DONE-WITH-YOU, qualifies DFY.
DFY ($2K-5K/mo): full agency execution, weekly 1:1, dedicated specialist, quarterly contracts → DONE-FOR-YOU, premium clients.

### Why the Ladder Changes DFY Sales
Without: client arrives cold, doesn't understand what you do, compares on price, close rate 10-20%, churns at 3-6 months.
With: client consumed content, understands framework, values execution, close rate 40-60%, retains 12+ months. Ticket jumps from $1,500-2,000 to $3,000-5,000 because they're not buying "marketing" — they're buying implementation of a system they understand.

### Revenue Projection ($10K formula)

Fixed costs: ~$199/mo (Skool + tools) + ad spend $1,200-1,500 = $1,400 total. Net: ~$9,500.

### Conversion Flow
Challenge leads/mo → 60-70% join free community → 10-15% upgrade to DIY → 15-25% of DIY upgrade to DWY → 10-20% of DWY become DFY.
Timeline: Month 1-2 setup + first leads. Month 3-4 first DWY. Month 5-6 first DFY conversations. Month 7-9: $10K+.

## Lead Magnet Strategy for Ecommerce
For store owners selling to agencies/consultants: challenge, audit, diagnostic tool.
For DTC brands selling to consumers: discount (most common but cheapest leads), quiz ("find your perfect [product]" — highest quality), guide/ebook (middle quality), free sample (high intent but costly), exclusive access/waitlist (scarcity).
Quiz funnel: 3-5 questions → personalized recommendation → email capture → result page with product CTA + discount. CVR 30-50% on quiz completion to email capture.
