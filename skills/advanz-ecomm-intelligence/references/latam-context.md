# LATAM Ecommerce Context — Chile, Mexico, Colombia

## Why LATAM Is Different
Trust barriers structurally higher than US/Europe. Ecommerce younger, fraud more visible in media. Culture of online buying still consolidating. What's "nice to have" in US (WhatsApp, physical address, multiple payment methods) is MANDATORY in LATAM.
CPMs 50-70% lower than US ($3-8 vs $10-20+). This is a massive advantage for ad-funded models.

## Payment Methods by Country

### Chile
Mandatory: Webpay (debit + credit — 40%+ transactions via debit), Mercado Pago, bank transfer.
Recommended: Khipu (direct transfer), Mach, prepaid cards.
BNPL: Mercado Pago cuotas (3-12 installments sin interés).
CRITICAL: Without debit card support, you block ~half of Chilean buyers. Webpay + Mercado Pago cuotas is the minimum.

### Mexico
Mandatory: credit/debit, OXXO (cash payment at convenience stores — massive for unbanked population), SPEI (bank transfer).
Recommended: Mercado Pago, PayPal, CoDi (QR).
BNPL: Kueski Pay, Aplazo, Mercado Pago cuotas.

### Colombia
Mandatory: PSE (bank transfer), credit/debit, Nequi (digital wallet), Efecty (cash payment).
Recommended: Bancolombia, Daviplata, PayU.
BNPL: Addi, Sistecrédito.

### US / Global
Mandatory: credit/debit, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
Recommended: Shop Pay (Shopify), Amazon Pay.
BNPL: Klarna, Afterpay, Shop Pay Installments.

## Legal Compliance

### Chile
Ley 19.496 (consumer protection), Ley 19.628 + Ley 21.719 (personal data).
Required: privacy policy, T&C, return policy (10-day minimum retracto by law), Google Consent Mode v2.

### Mexico
LFPC (consumer protection), LFPDPPP (data protection).
Required: privacy notice (mandatory by law), T&C, return policy, RFC + physical address visible.

### Colombia
Ley 1480 (consumer statute), Ley 1581 (personal data).
Required: data treatment policy, T&C, retracto (5 days), NIT visible.

### Global (Shopify)
GDPR (EU sales), CCPA (California). Cookie banner, GDPR-compliant privacy, data opt-out.

**CRITICAL: Published policies are required BEFORE running ads. Meta and Google may reject domain verification or limit accounts without them.**

## WhatsApp in LATAM
95%+ connected population uses WA daily. Open rate 90%+. It's not "a channel" — it's THE channel.
Must have: WA button visible on store (header + PDP), response time <5 min during business hours, auto-reply outside hours.
For sales: Click-to-WhatsApp Ads work for high-consideration products in LATAM where trust is low and customers want to "talk to someone real" before buying.
For support: expected as minimum. Brands without WA visible feel untrustworthy.

## Shipping in LATAM
Biggest complaint: uncertainty. "When does it arrive?" is the #1 question.
Must communicate: estimated delivery days on PDP, tracking link in confirmation email + WhatsApp, proactive updates at dispatch and delivery.
Free shipping threshold: very effective in LATAM. Set at 1.3× AOV.

## Trust Building in LATAM (checklist)
1. WhatsApp visible with response <1h ✓
2. Physical address somewhere on site (footer) ✓
3. Return policy in simple language (not legal jargon) ✓
4. ALL local payment methods active ✓
5. BNPL/cuotas visible next to price ✓
6. Shipping calculator before checkout ✓
7. Reviews visible on PDP (social proof) ✓
8. "About Us" with real photos of real people ✓
9. SSL + payment security badges at checkout ✓
10. Published privacy policy and T&C ✓
Without these 10, expect 30-50% higher abandonment vs a store that has them all.

## Currency Considerations
Chile: CLP. Mexico: MXN. Colombia: COP. All have high nominal numbers (a $50 USD product can be $48,000 CLP or $1,000 MXN or $215,000 COP). Display prices in local currency ALWAYS. Use Shopify Markets for multi-currency.

## Cultural Notes
Chilean consumers value: transparency, simplicity, proof (reviews), and being able to "talk to someone."
Mexican consumers value: cash payment options (OXXO), installments, brand presence on social media.
Colombian consumers value: flexible payment (Nequi, Efecty), fast WhatsApp responses, explicit guarantees.
