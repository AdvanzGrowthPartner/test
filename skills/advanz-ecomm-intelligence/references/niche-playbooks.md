# Niche Playbooks — Supplements, Pets, Skincare, Sportswear

All chosen for: high recompra, community potential, brand-love, margin. Explicitly excluded: commodities, low-recompra, no community potential.

## Supplements / Longevidad

### Profile
Consumable (monthly cycle). AOV $50-75. Repeat rate excellent (30-45%). LTV high via stacking. Community: biohackers, fitness, wellness seekers.

### Key Differentiators That Win
Bioavailability data (form matters: bisglycinate > oxide). Clinical dosage (not pixie-dusted). Third-party testing certificates. Ingredient transparency (full INCI list). Stack logic (products designed to work together).

### Social Proof That Converts
Reviews with MEASURABLE results: "I sleep better since week 2", "my bloodwork improved". Not generic "great product." Guide reviews: "What problem did you have? What result did you see? How long?"
Certifications: lab testing, GMP, NSF. These matter more in supplements than any other niche.

### Cross-sell Map
Magnesium → D3+K2 (work together). Omega-3 → D3 (both fat-soluble, absorb better together). Protein → creatine/aminos (training progression). Any individual → bundle "Stack Básico" (15-20% off).

### Content Strategy
Top articles: "How to choose the best [supplement]", "[Form A] vs [Form B]", "Symptoms of [nutrient] deficiency", "Daily supplementation routine", "FAQ: dosage, timing, combinations."

### Subscription
10-15% off monthly auto-ship. Cycle: 30 days. Churn: 8-12%/mo. Key: show per-day cost ("less than $1.50/day").

### Replenishment Email
60 caps (2/day) → 30d cycle → email day 24.

## Mascotas Premium

### Profile
Consumable (monthly). AOV $45-70. Repeat rate best of all niches (35-50%). LTV very high via subscription. Community: passionate pet owners = organic UGC machine.

### Key Differentiators
Clean ingredient list (no subproducts, no fillers). Breed/size-specific formulations. Transparency in sourcing. Vet-recommended or vet-formulated claims. Sustainability/eco packaging resonates.

### Social Proof That Converts
Reviews BY BREED AND SIZE: "My 3-year-old labrador loves it." + photo of pet eating. Not "good food." Number of subscribers/families: "5,000 families trust us."
CRITICAL: Certifications without emotional context don't work in pets. Owners want to see HAPPY PETS, not lab reports.

### Cross-sell Map
Food → dental snacks (complementary nutrition). Snacks → joint supplement (for adult/senior dogs). Food + snacks → monthly subscription box. Any → seasonal items (cooling mat summer, coat supplement winter).

### Content Strategy
Top articles: "Natural vs commercial food: real differences", "Signs of food intolerance in dogs", "How much should your dog eat by breed/weight", "Ingredients to avoid", "How to transition to natural food."

### Subscription
10% off + free shipping. Cycle: 30-45 days (adjustable by weight). Churn: 6-10%/mo (lowest of all niches). Monthly box concept very strong.

### UGC Gold
Pet photos = engagement gold. Hashtag campaign. Monthly "pet of the month" contest. Owners share compulsively. This niche has the strongest organic UGC engine.

## Skincare Natural

### Profile
Consumable (45-75 day cycle). AOV $55-85. Repeat rate good (25-40%). LTV high via routine expansion (1 product → 3-step routine → 5-step). Community: skincare enthusiasts share routines.

### Key Differentiators
Ingredient transparency (full INCI + explanations). Certifications (ECOCERT, cruelty-free, vegan). Results timeline ("visible in 14 days"). Skin type specificity (not one-size-fits-all). Clean formulation story.

### Social Proof That Converts
Reviews with SKIN TYPE + BEFORE/AFTER: "Combination skin, 38 years old, used for 3 weeks, visible difference." Not "nice cream."
The friction: "Will it work for MY skin?" Reviews that answer this by skin type are 10x more valuable.

### Cross-sell Map
Serum → moisturizer (next step in routine). Cleanser + serum → full 3-step kit (15% off). Any facial → mineral sunscreen (most-missing step). Individual → routine bundle "Complete your routine."

### Content Strategy
Top articles: "3-step skincare routine for beginners", "Ingredients to avoid", "Vitamin C: how it really works", "How to know your skin type", "Correct application order."

### Subscription
10% off + free shipping. Eco-refill option (lower price, less packaging). Cycle: 45-60 days. Churn: 10-15%/mo.

### Replenishment
Serum 30ml (~0.5ml/use/day): 60d cycle → email day 48. Moisturizer 50ml (~1ml/use, 2x/day): 25d → email day 20.

## Ropa Deportiva / Activewear

### Profile
NON-consumable. AOV $65-90. Repeat rate moderate (20-30%). LTV via new collections, not replenishment. Community: fitness, yoga, running communities.

### Key Differentiators
Fit documentation: real customers, real measurements, real photos. Fabric technology explained (not just "premium"). Sustainability story. Size inclusivity. Designed for specific activity (not generic "activewear").

### Social Proof That Converts
Reviews that mention SIZE + BODY TYPE + FIT: "I'm 1.65m, bought M, squats test perfect, no transparency." Not "nice legging."
UGC of real women training. Not models. Not studio. Real gyms, real bodies, real workouts.

### Recompra Triggers (NOT replenishment)
New collection launches. Seasonal colors. Complete the outfit (bought legging → top). Limited editions. Community challenges ("30-day challenge" → gear up).

### Cross-sell Map
1 legging → legging + top set (15% off). Any item → new collection early access. Running gear → cold weather add-ons (seasonal).

### Content Strategy
Top articles: "Size guide with real measurements by body type", "Best fabrics for training", "How to build your workout outfit", "How long does sportswear last", "What to look for in quality leggings."

### Subscription Alternative
Not traditional subscription (you don't need new leggings monthly). Instead: "VIP quarterly box" — curated seasonal piece, early access, exclusive colorways. Or simply: loyalty program with points per purchase + seasonal notifications.

### The Fit Problem
#1 barrier to purchase AND #1 reason for returns in sportswear. Solution: exhaustive size guide with body photos (not size chart alone), reviews that mention specific measurements, free exchange policy prominently displayed, "fit quiz" (3 questions → size recommendation).
