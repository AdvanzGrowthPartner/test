# Organic Growth — SEO, GEO/AIO, Content, Social Media

## SEO for Ecommerce — 3 Pillars (in priority order)

### 1. Technical SEO (do first)
Core Web Vitals: LCP <2.5s, FID <100ms, CLS <0.1. Each extra second of load = -2% CVR.
Mobile-first indexing: Google indexes mobile version first. Score 90+ on PageSpeed mobile.
Sitemap XML: submitted to Search Console. Include pages to index, exclude admin/cart/checkout.
Clean URLs: /collections/suplementos/magnesio, not query params. Canonical tags to avoid Shopify duplicates (/collections/all/).
robots.txt: don't block indexable pages. SSL 100% HTTPS. Fix 404s with 301 redirects.
Schema markup: Product, FAQ, Review/AggregateRating, Breadcrumb, Organization, HowTo. JSON-LD in <head>.
Shopify resolves ~70% of technical SEO by default (SSL, mobile, auto sitemap). Focus effort on: speed (theme + apps), canonicals, Schema, URLs.

### 2. On-Page SEO (do second)
Title tag (50-60 chars): [Keyword] + [Benefit] + [Brand].
Meta description (150-160 chars): [Benefit] + [Differentiator] + [CTA].
H1: unique per page, contains primary keyword naturally.
H2s: subtopics covering objections and secondary keywords.
Alt text: [Keyword] + [specific visual description].
URL slug: short, descriptive, keyword. No numbers/params.
Unique descriptions: NOT copied from supplier. Each PDP unique.
Internal linking: PDPs → collections, blog → PDPs, blog → blog.
CRITICAL ERROR: same auto-generated meta description on all PDPs = duplicate content. Write unique metas for top 10 pages minimum (2-3 hours of work, months of impact).

### 3. Content Editorial (do third)
Blog is not for "having a blog." It captures searches from people in Investigation/Evaluation phase and leads them to your product.
Content types by intent: buying guide (comparison), comparativa (A vs B), tutorial/how-to (education), listicle/common errors (awareness), FAQ with Schema (all intents), glossary/educational (authority).
Every article MUST have CTA to product/collection. No CTA = traffic that reads and leaves.
2-4 articles/month well-optimized > 20 generic articles.
Schema FAQ on articles: AI engines and Google both favor Q&A format.

## GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) — 2026

### What It Is
SEO = rank in a list of search results to get clicks. GEO = be CITED inside AI-generated answers. Different game. SEO drives traffic. GEO shapes perception inside the answer itself.
Platforms: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude.

### Why It Matters Now
31.3% US using AI search (EMARKETER 2026). 60% searches zero-click. AI traffic to retail +4,700% YoY. Cited brands: +38% clicks, +39% paid ad performance. Gartner: -25% organic by end 2026. GEO results appear in 30-60 days (faster than SEO 6-12 months).

### 7 Principles for Ecommerce GEO
1. **Direct answers**: Each section starts with clear, extractable statement. Not vague marketing copy.
2. **Citable data**: Specific numbers, percentages, comparisons. "97% botanical ingredients" > "natural ingredients".
3. **Clear hierarchy**: H2/H3 well-defined. Each section stands alone. TL;DR at start of long sections.
4. **FAQ sections with Schema**: LLMs' preferred format for extraction. Exact question + direct answer.
5. **Brand entity authority**: LLMs cite brands they recognize. Build presence on Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, Wikipedia/Wikidata.
6. **Original research**: Proprietary data, benchmarks, frameworks nobody else has. "We surveyed 500 dog owners and 73% didn't know…"
7. **Content freshness**: Visible "Last updated" timestamps. Refresh cornerstone content every 3-6 months.

### Where LLMs Pull Sources From (build presence here)
Reddit: 100M+ DAU, one of most-cited sources. Participate genuinely in niche subreddits. NO brand spam.
YouTube: Transcriptions indexed as authority content. Educational videos with optimized titles/descriptions.
LinkedIn: Growing as professional expertise source. Founder/team publishing niche content.
Wikipedia/Wikidata: Entity recognition for Knowledge Graph. Contribute factual data (no promotion).
Earned media: PR, mentions, interviews. LLMs prioritize third-party over brand-owned. A mention in a nutrition blog > 10 blog posts on your site.

### GEO ≠ SEO Replacement
GEO complements SEO. 70% of searches still traditional. Good news: much of what works for SEO also works for GEO (structure, clarity, authority). Budget recommendation (Search Engine Land): 40% core SEO, 25% digital PR, 20% data/reporting, 10% training, 5% experimentation.

## Social Media Organic for Ecommerce

### Platform Strategy
Instagram: primary for DTC visual brands. Reels 2-3x reach of static. UGC reshare builds community. 3-5 posts/week + daily stories.
TikTok: highest organic reach potential. Native unpolished content wins. 3-7 posts/week for algorithm favor.
Pinterest: underrated for ecommerce. Long shelf life. Great for skincare, home, fashion. SEO within Pinterest.
LinkedIn: B2B and founder personal brand. Content from the "why" behind the brand.
Reddit: community participation, not marketing. Answer questions in niche subreddits. Builds GEO visibility.

### Content Pillars for Ecomm (4-pillar model)
1. **Educational** (40%): How-tos, tips, ingredient/material explainers, myth-busting.
2. **Social proof** (25%): UGC reshares, reviews, before/after, customer stories.
3. **Behind the scenes** (20%): Production, team, values, brand story, packaging.
4. **Promotional** (15%): Product launches, offers, bundles. ONLY 15% — if more, you lose the algorithm and the audience.

### UGC System
Hashtag unique to brand. Incentivize with discount/points/free product. Reshare best UGC in feed + stories (with permission). Use UGC as ad creative (converts better than produced). Micro-ambassador program: 10-20 top clients with product + exclusive code. In community niches (pets, sport, wellness), UGC IS the growth engine.
