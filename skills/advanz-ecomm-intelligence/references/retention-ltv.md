# Retention & LTV — Repeat Purchase, Subscriptions, Loyalty, Referrals

## The Second Purchase Problem
Acquiring new customer costs 5-7x more than retaining. A 2-time buyer has dramatically higher probability of buying 3, 4, 5 times. If second purchase doesn't happen in 60-90 days, probability drops sharply. If repeat rate <15% after 12 months: STOP scaling ads. You're in "leaky bucket" mode.

## Golden Window: 0-90 Days Post-Purchase
Day 0: flawless confirmation + tracking. Day 1-3: confirm delivery + catch problems. Day 3-7: usage guide (KEY — if they don't use it right, no results, no review, no recompra). Day 7-14: request review + first cross-sell. Day 14-30: value content + second purchase offer. Day 30-45: replenishment reminder for consumables (cycle × 0.8). Day 45-60: exclusive "come back" offer with deadline. Day 60-90: aggressive win-back (best discount). Day 90+: cold list, reduce frequency, reactivate quarterly.

## 5 Barriers to Second Purchase
1. "Forgot the brand" → no post-purchase communication. Solution: content, retargeting, social.
2. "Don't need more of same" → no cross-sell. Solution: recommend next logical step.
3. "Experience wasn't wow" → generic packaging, slow shipping. Solution: improve unboxing, day 3 guide.
4. "Expensive to rebuy" → no loyalty program. Solution: points, subscription discount, repeat incentive.
5. "Nothing new" → static catalog. Solution: launches, seasonal editions, fresh content.

## Cross-sell Rules
Recommend by RESULT logic, not volume. The recommended product must improve the result the customer is already seeking. If no result logic → feels like spam.
Supplements: magnesio → D3+K2 (work together), protein → creatine (training progression), individual → stack bundle.
Pets: food → dental snacks (complementary nutrition), snacks → joint supplement (owner who cares about nutrition also cares about long-term health), food+snacks → monthly subscription box.
Skincare: serum → moisturizer (completes routine), cleanser+serum → full 3-step kit, any facial → mineral sunscreen.
Sport: 1 legging → legging+top set, any item → new collection access.

## Replenishment Flow (consumables)
Timing = product cycle × 0.8 (arrive BEFORE it runs out).
Supplements 60 caps (2/day): 30d cycle → email day 24. Skincare serum 30ml: 60d → email day 48. Pet food 3kg: 30d → email day 24. Snacks 30 units: 20d → email day 16.
For non-consumables (sportswear): trigger = new collection launch, seasonal, not "your legging is wearing out."

## Subscription Model (Subscribe & Save)
Discount: 10-15% off vs one-time. Free shipping always. Easy to pause/cancel (MANDATORY — difficult cancellation = resentment + negative reviews, not retention).
Trial-to-paid: 15-25%. Monthly churn target: <10%. Best niches: supplements (8-12% churn), pets (6-10%, lowest churn due to high need), skincare (10-15%), sportswear quarterly box (15-20%/quarter).
Apps: Recharge, Bold Subscriptions, Shopify Subscriptions native.

## VIP / Loyalty Program (3 Tiers)
Bronze (entry, 0-99 pts): early access to sales + 1 pt per $1 spent. Silver (100-499 pts, ~3-5 purchases): free shipping always + 5% permanent discount + 1.5x points. Gold (500+ pts, ~8-10 purchases): free product per semester + 10% off + launch access + priority support.
Points must be SIMPLE: 1 pt = $1 spent. 100 pts = $X off. If you need a calculator, it's too complex.
Communicate in post-purchase emails, packaging, account page. Apps: Smile.io, LoyaltyLion, Yotpo Loyalty.

## Referral Program
Structure: "Give $X, Get $X" — bilateral incentive. Referrer gets credit on next purchase. Referred gets discount on first.
Timing: activate at peak satisfaction — day 7-10 post-delivery or after 5-star review. NOT at random.
By niche: Supplements ($5-8 credit / 15% off), Sport ($10 credit / $10 off), Pets ($5-7 / 15% off), Skincare ($7 credit or mini product / 10% off + free sample).
Referred customers have 16% higher LTV and convert at higher rate due to pre-built trust.
Track: CAC of referrals vs CAC of ads monthly. Referral CAC should be 50-80% lower.

## NPS (Net Promoter Score)
Formula: % Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6). Scale: -100 to 100.
<0: critical. 0-30: acceptable. 30-50: good. 50-70: excellent. 70+: world class.
If NPS <30: no marketing compensates. Fix product/service first.
If NPS >50: license to scale referrals and community aggressively.
Actions: 9-10 → referral invite + review request. 7-8 → review request (no referral). 0-6 → CX alert, personal contact BEFORE they leave public negative review.
